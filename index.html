Short Bio

Lazhar Labiod is an Assistant Professor at the IUT de Paris – Rives de Seine, Université Paris Cité, and a researcher affiliated with the Centre Borelli. He received his Ph.D. in Statistics from Pierre and Marie Curie University (Paris 6), France.

His research focuses on machine learning and data mining, with particular emphasis on data embedding and co-clustering methods. His work spans biomedical data analysis, tensor and attributed graph co-clustering, recommender systems, and multi-view data mining.

His research has been published in leading venues: IEEE TNNLS, IEEE TKDE, Pattern Recognition, WSDM, ICDM, CIKM, and SIGIR.

Profile & Career

Lazhar Labiod is an Associate Professor (Maître de conférences) in Computer Science at Université Paris Cité, where he has been teaching and conducting research since 2013. He is affiliated with the Centre Borelli (UMR 9010, CNRS) within the AI-DSCy research team and teaches at the IUT Paris Rives de Seine.

He received his MSc in Statistics from Université Pierre et Marie Curie in 2003 and earned a PhD in Applied Mathematics (Statistics), focusing on relational frameworks for simultaneous clustering methods. Following his PhD, he held several postdoctoral positions in machine learning and data science, including appointments at the Laboratoire d'Informatique de Paris Nord and LIPADE, as well as a research collaboration between TotalEnergies and AgroParisTech.

His research focuses on the design of advanced machine learning methods for the analysis of complex data, with particular emphasis on unsupervised learning, co-clustering, representation learning, attributed graph learning, and multi-view data embedding. These contributions aim to improve the interpretability and scalability of data-driven models in domains such as text mining, network analysis, and biomedical data science. In addition to his research activities, he plays an active role in data science education and currently serves as academic coordinator of the Master's program in Machine Learning for Data Science (MLSD). He obtained his Habilitation to Supervise Research (HDR) in 2025.

Research Themes

Methods
Joint Learning Paradigms
Unified frameworks simultaneously integrating dimensionality reduction and clustering for attribute data, simple graphs, and attributed graphs.
Networks
Attributed Graph Learning
Modelling interdependencies in large-scale networks via affinity matrices and Graph Convolution, fusing topology and node attributes.
Clustering
Regularised Co-clustering
Extending simultaneous learning to regularised co-clustering with semantic enrichment, applied to Text Mining and biomedical data.
Multi-way
Tensor Modelling & LBM
Algorithms for high-dimensional tensor co-clustering, adapting the Latent Block Model to various distributions for recommender systems.
Robustness
Ensemble Learning
Novel co-clustering ensemble framework generating robust consensus partitions insensitive to initialisation and number of clusters.
Applications
Biomedical Data Mining
Omics data analysis, medical NLP, and heterogeneous data mining within ANR and ANSES-funded projects (GePhEx, MOLDASTH, MIMS).
